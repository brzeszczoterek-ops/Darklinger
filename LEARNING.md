# Darklinger Public learning

Generated tools and skills pass validation before activation and execute under the restricted client sandbox policy. Declaring a capability in generated source does not grant it.

Persistent promotion requires validated evidence and the applicable owner approvals. Runtime outcomes, validation failures, and artifact history are recorded locally. Reports must distinguish a tested artifact from a proposed or rejected one.

Use `darklinger-learning verify`, `darklinger-learning artifacts`, `darklinger-learning evidence --limit 50`, and `darklinger-learning lessons` to inspect the store.

See [Generated-tool validation](docs/generated-tool-validation.md) for fixture scope and remaining limitations. Runtime data and generated personal artifacts are not shipped in the distribution.
